# Admissibility Theory Authority Boundary

## Primary role

`admissibility-theory` is authoritative for scientific and epistemic claim-entry conditions: discriminability, specification, boundary conditions, chain-of-custody criteria, and the maturity/admission framework defined by this research program.

## Research independence

This repository is an upstream research program. It is not required to consume the controlled AURUM-V canonical upstream (`AURUMV_CONTROLLED_CANONICAL_UPSTREAM`) merely in order to develop or review its theory.

Its research conclusions do not automatically alter production governance, execution permissions, or kernel state semantics.

## Explicit non-authority

This repository is not authoritative for:

- AURUM-V admissibility-state definitions;
- kernel invariants;
- canonical state-transition contracts;
- execution authority;
- constitutional authority;
- domain evidence truth;
- ranking or truth adjudication after a framework has passed the admission gate;
- independent certification.

## Translation rule

If a result from Admissibility Theory is proposed for operational use in AURUM-V, it must enter through an explicit governed kernel proposal, review, versioned contract change, and downstream conformance process.

Research publication or repository merge alone does not change production behavior.
