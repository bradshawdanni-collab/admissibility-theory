# Security Policy

## Reporting

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities publicly. Report them privately to the repository owner through GitHub private vulnerability reporting when available, or another private contact method published by the owner.

Include the affected component, reproduction steps, potential impact, and supporting evidence. Do not access, alter, retain, or disclose third-party or production data while testing.

## Scope

Reports may concern source code, dependencies, authentication, authorization, cryptographic verification, data exposure, or documented deployment interfaces.

Reports are assessed according to reproducibility, exploitability, and potential impact. Acknowledgement or remediation timing is not guaranteed.
